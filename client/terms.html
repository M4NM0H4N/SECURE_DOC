What the service is

DocFlow lets students of an institution ask for official documents and lets that institution's staff review, approve and issue them. Each institution is a separate space; the operator provides the software, and the institution decides who may use it and how requests are handled.

Your account

Keep your password and any security codes private. You are responsible for what is done with your account. Tell your institution's Admin at once if you think someone else has used it.

Acceptable use

Do not upload malware, attempt to access another institution's or person's data, probe or overload the service, or use it for anything unlawful. Uploaded files are scanned; files that fail the scan are refused.

Documents and decisions

Approval and issue of documents is decided by the institution, not by the operator. A verification code on an issued document shows whether the institution issued that exact file and whether it has since been revoked.
