What is held

Your name, e-mail address, role and department; your document requests (type, purpose, status, history); documents issued to you; an audit trail of actions on those records; and security data (a password hash, sign-in attempts, an optional two-step sign-in secret stored encrypted).

Why

To run the document workflow for your institution, keep it secure, and notify you of progress. The institution decides the purposes; the operator processes the data on its behalf.

Who sees it

Only people of your institution with a role that needs it (for example the clerk and head of department handling your request), and the operator's staff where needed to run the service. Other institutions cannot see it. Analytics and the integration API contain counts and statuses, not names or purposes.

You may ask your institution for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. The institution's Admin can download all of its data, and the operator deletes an institution's data on its written request.
