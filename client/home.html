How it works

  1. 1

    Request

    A student asks for an official document and states the purpose. It is logged at once.

  2. 2

    Review

    A clerk of the student's department checks the request and moves it forward.

  3. 3

    Approve

    The head of department approves, or rejects with a written reason the student can read.

  4. 4

    Issue & verify

    The clerk uploads the document. The student downloads it, and anyone can verify it with its code or QR.

Three layers of trust

Identity Who is acting?

Sign-in with optional two-step codes, fixed roles, and department scope: staff only ever see their own department.

Validation Is it allowed?

Only legal status steps are accepted, a rejection needs a reason, and uploads are checked by their real file type.

Proof Can it be verified?

Every action goes into an audit log chained with hashes, so tampering is detectable. Issued documents carry a signed verification code.
