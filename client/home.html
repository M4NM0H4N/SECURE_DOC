      One system, five roles

      Student

      • Register and request any official document, with the purpose
      • Track every request: status, history and the reason if it is rejected
      • Answer a reviewer's question and resubmit
      • Download the issued document and verify it with its code or QR

      Clerk

      • Work a queue limited to your own department
      • Move a request to review, or ask the student for more information
      • Upload the official document once it is approved
      • Replace or withdraw a document that was issued by mistake

      Head of department

      • See only the requests of your own department
      • Approve, or reject with a written reason the student can read
      • Every decision is recorded with your name and the time

      Principal

      • Take the final decision where your institution's chain includes it
      • See institution-wide analytics: turnaround times, backlog, rejection rate

      Admin

      • Invite staff, deactivate leavers, unlock accounts
      • Set departments, document types and the approval chain
      • Read the audit trail and check its integrity in one click
      • Export data, set branding, and create keys and webhooks for integrations

      Three layers of trust

      Identity Who is acting?

      Sign-in with optional two-step codes, fixed roles, and department scope: staff only ever see their own department.

      Validation Is it allowed?

      Only legal status steps are accepted, a rejection needs a reason, and uploads are checked by their real file type and scanned.

      Proof Can it be verified?

      Every action goes into an audit log chained with hashes, so tampering is detectable. Issued documents carry a signed verification code.

      Questions, answered

      Who can see my request?

      You, and staff of your own department whose role needs it. Other departments and other institutions cannot see it. The Admin sees the list but cannot read documents.

      Are my documents private?

      Yes. Files are stored privately and only the right people can download them through the system. They are checked by their real file type and scanned when scanning is enabled.

      Can someone check a document without signing in?

      Yes. Every issued document has a verification code and QR. The verify page tells you whether it is genuine, replaced or withdrawn.

      Is this a blockchain?

      No. Actions are recorded in an audit log chained with hashes, which makes tampering detectable. It is not a blockchain and it does not replace the institution's own records.

      What if my request is rejected?

      The head of department must write a reason, and you can read it on your request. A reviewer can also ask you for more information, and you can answer and resubmit.

      Will I get e-mails?

      If your institution has turned e-mail on, you are notified about progress. You can also see everything in the notifications page.
